## About Me

I am a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia DKI, a member of UCLA's Code Intelligence (COIN) Lab, and a member of Pasta Lab. My research focuses on building capable and trustworthy coding agents through realistic execution environments, verifiable code synthesis and repair, and efficient learning for agentic AI. I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UC San Diego.

## Current Research

- **Environment engineering for coding agents:** realistic tasks, executable environments, and task corpora for training and evaluation.
- **Verifiable code synthesis and repair:** retrieval, execution feedback, and automated verification in the loop.
- **Efficient agentic learning:** resource-efficient training, reinforcement learning, and inference for AI agents.
